## Key dimensions (Primus FX105, the model we follow)
- H x W x D: 1245 x 795 x 795 mm; drum diameter 620 mm, 105 l, 12 kg.
- Door centre 648 mm above floor; dark door surround reaches down to ~342 mm.
- Control strip 180 mm tall across the full width; feet clearance 48 mm.
- Drain valve 76 mm outer diameter (rear, low); 3/4" water inlets at rear top.

## Visual details
- Anthracite painted front and side panels, stainless steel top and control strip.
- Control strip slightly tilted: red "central stop" button at left, 7" touchscreen,
  small brand badge at right.
- Door: thick light-grey ring (~62% of width) set in a large dark recessed
  surround; round knob handle on the left, hinges hidden on the right;
  deep glass bowl; a blue ring visible just inside the glass.
- Sides: large vertical rounded-rectangle embossed panel.
- Soap hopper on top, towards the back.
- Stainless cabinet is an option (FXB barrier models are full stainless).

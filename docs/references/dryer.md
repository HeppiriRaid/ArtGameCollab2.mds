## Primus DX16 (the model we follow)
- H x W x D 1625 x 800 x 1155 mm (depth includes the rear exhaust; body ~960).
- Drum 762 mm, galvanised perforated, 350 l; exhaust 150-200 mm.
- Stainless front panel, black painted round door with big glass window,
  hinges on the right; door centre roughly 0.95 m up.
- Full-width black bar handle across the lint drawer below the door (~0.57 m).
- Top: black 7" touchscreen pod near the centre, small brand badge left.
